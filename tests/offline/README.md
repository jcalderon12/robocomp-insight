# Offline tests (no robot / no Webots required)

These tests exercise the semantic ↔ inner_simulator integration pipeline without
launching the agent stack. Run them from the repo root.

| Test | What it checks | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `test_validator.py` | `LiveCausalValidator` decision table (unexplained vs. explained retractions): a bottle that leaves the robot without a cause is unexplained with one reason wherever it went; and the mirror never places a lost bottle (no `hasLocation` room even if DSR hangs it from the room). | rdflib |
| `test_blueprint_grounding.py` | Blueprint v2 validation and grounding against `etc/intervention_catalog.json` (testable / untestable marking, bounds). | rdflib |
| `test_hypothesis_compiler.py` | Semantic batch → `hypothesis_compiler` → payloads accepted by the causes simulator's discriminated union. | pybullet |
| `test_verdict_ground_truth.py` | **Slow (~1-2 min).** Full contrastive loop with injected ground truth: a simulated `external_force` episode (a gentle, realistic push) acts as the real one; the verdict must accept it and reject bump/wheel/friction/nominal; the semantic ingestor derives the causal triples. | pybullet |
| `test_scene_and_verdict_safeguards.py` | (~10 s) Replay heading (DSR +y-forward vs URDF +x-forward), observed effect and horizon cut, setpoints cut at the observed effect, anomaly window anchored on it, and the verdict safeguards: abstention when the nominal run reproduces the effect (also in the semantic ingestor), blown-up physics, obstacles spawned overlapping the robot, implausible landings, deterministic tie-break; and the episode clock: the rate fitted from the recording (travelled over ordered) and the simulator replaying on it while recording on the recording's clock. | pybullet |
| `test_insight_tbox.py` | (~15 s) The INSIGHT TBox (`agents/semantic/data/insight_tbox.ttl`) matches its sources, keeps the SOMA/DUL/SOSA hierarchy the contracts rely on, declares the ten mechanisms consistently with `etc/intervention_catalog.json` and every term production writes, and answers queries over a real mirrored state. HermiT consistency check when owlready2 is installed. | rdflib (owlready2 optional) |
| `test_episode_builder.py` | (~20 s) The episode of contract 1 (`agents/semantic/src/episode_builder.py`): the core on a synthetic recording (segments counted back from the fall, phases from the first motion, peaks in the fall interval, nothing after t_obs but the marked reaction); the 12:29 recording of 2026-09-24, read with `experiments/episode_series.py`, gives the contract's worked example; the five falls of that session build a consistent episode whose fall interval holds the largest pitch rate before the fall (the robot stalls against the bump in two of them), and the production reader (`episode_memory_reader.py`, episodic-memory API) gives the same one as the file reader; the episode in RDF (`episode_rdf.py`) only uses TBox terms and answers when and where the bottle fell; HermiT finds the TBox plus the episode consistent; the tray (`PhysicalObject_Tray`, a `soma:DesignedComponent` of the robot) holds the bottle. | numpy, rdflib; the recordings and `experiments/` (not in git); pydsr + episodic_memory_api and owlready2 optional (skipped without them) |
| `test_episode_contrast.py` | (~1 s) The contrast of the mechanisms with the episode (`agents/semantic/src/episode_contrast.py`): the preconditions and contrast rules of each mechanism are read from the TBox as contract 2 lists them, and a TBox with a check the module does not implement (or reading other properties) is refused; on a hand-made episode each check lands on the right side of its threshold, a missing value never discards, the system's reaction (the braking after the fall) is never read, a rule says nothing about anchors its evidence does not cover, and the recorded turn is compared with the ordered one scaled by the episode's own clock (so turning in free motion under the Webots clock does not keep a wheel failure alive); the 12:29 recording of 2026-09-24 gives the contract's column of table 3.6, and in the five falls of that session (all with the bump) `obstacle_traversed` is never discarded. | rdflib, numpy; the recordings and `experiments/` (not in git) |
| `test_hypothesis_pipeline.py` | (~1 s) From the hypotheses in symbols to the published batch (`agents/semantic/src/hypothesis_pipeline.py`, contract 3): the qualitative parameters, titles and catalog interventions come from the TBox, and a TBox with other values than the grounding translates is refused; a list that breaks the schema (unknown mechanism, parameter outside its list or missing, a magnitude, any number) is refused as a whole, while a wrong anchor or a false precondition only makes that hypothesis incoherent; on a hand-made episode the grounding gives the numbers of contract 2 (a bump is every bump of the catalog, one cause each; obstacle box, cable only across x, activation window, the whole force range by direction), a wheel of unknown side is two causes, and the budget stops at the first hypothesis that does not fit, and without a budget (production since 2026-10-07) every hypothesis that survives and can be simulated is; with the production catalog, which has the dome of unknown size (`spawn_scaled_dome`), a bump is one hypothesis of cost 4 with the area and the whole size range, and the simulator's cause draws 36 domes within them (with a catalog of fixed bumps only, one cause per asset, as before); the six ideas of the real 12:29 batch give the states and window of contract 3, and in the five falls of 2026-09-24 the anchored enumeration puts the true bump's centre inside its box; every blueprint passes the `hypothesis_service` validation, the simulator's compiler takes the batch unchanged and the causes simulator accepts the causes. | rdflib, numpy; the recordings and `experiments/` (not in git); pybullet optional |
| `test_hypothesis_generator.py` | (~1 s) The LLM in front of the published batch (`agents/semantic/src/hypothesis_generator.py`), with a scripted LLM (no network): the prompt of the 12:29 fall gives the episode with every anchor a hypothesis may take (not the system's reaction after the fall), the mechanisms from the TBox, the self-model once and, only with a budget k, the budget and the costs (production's `Budget = "all"` reads as no budget), and no number of the grounding; an answer that is not JSON or breaks the schema is sent back with its problems and asked again, a failure to reach the LLM is asked again as it was, and the attempts stay in the batch; with no valid answer the batch has status `error` and only the nominal run is compiled; the batch is saved with its prompt and the whole conversation. | rdflib, numpy; the recordings and `experiments/` (not in git) |
| `test_semantic_agent_v2.py` | (~5 s) The semantic agent with the generation v2, without robot: its own code (`specificworker.py`) runs on a simulated DSR (working and episodic graphs), a simulated GraphDB and a scripted LLM over the 12:29 recording. It waits for the recording as the inner simulator does, builds with the episodic-memory API the same episode as the file reader and keeps it in its own named graph, publishes a 3b batch on the `hypotheses_filepath` attribute that the simulator's compiler takes, ignores a verdict that does not answer its batch, and with the verdict that accepts the bump writes the verified mechanism (`obstacle_traversed` on `Segment_final`, title from the mechanism) in the episode's graph, while the live graph gets what it got before and loses nothing. GraphDB keeps only the current case: a case starts by putting the live graph back to the mirror alone and dropping previous episode graphs, and a second case (12:40) finds nothing of the first. What the memory decides stays in the memory (contracts 1.8): the INSIGHT TBox goes to its own graph at start-up (or at the next case if GraphDB was down) and no reset touches it; every hypothesis is in the episode's graph with the batch's status and the check (and observation) that decided it; the verdict adds the nominal and simulated runs, also when nothing is accepted, and the verified cause causes the accident; the episode's graph only uses TBox terms. It runs under a decimal-comma numeric locale, as the agent does under Qt, and checks that every slot connected to a DSR signal carries the annotations pydsr routes by (both faults of the first Webots run, 06/10). | PySide6, pydsr + episodic_memory_api, Ice, RoboComp's ConfigLoader (skipped without them); the recordings and `experiments/` (not in git) |
| `test_semantic_memory_queries.py` | (~40 s) The competency questions of the semantic memory (`agents/semantic/data/queries/cq1`–`cq9`, contracts 1.8) on the Webots run of 2026-10-07 at 10:00: its episode and batch as the agent wrote them and the verdict that accepts the bump (`tests/offline/data/`), rebuilt into the named graphs the agent leaves in GraphDB (`memory_queries.case_dataset`). Each question gives what the files give: when and where the bottle fell, what the LLM proposed and the memory decided, which check and observation discarded each hypothesis (H05: `person_within_reach` on 3.269 m), which mechanisms leave no trace on any sensor, the verified cause (H02 caused `Accident_1`, 7 of 36 repetitions; the nominal 0 of 10) with its sampled magnitudes, the simulation runs, what cannot be simulated and the provenance. Only TBox terms; HermiT finds the TBox plus the whole case consistent. The same questions on the real GraphDB: `experiments/semantic_memory_graphdb.py`. | rdflib (owlready2 optional) |
| `test_scaled_dome.py` | (~15 s) The bump of unknown size in the causes simulator (`spawn_scaled_dome`, `causes/implementations/cause_bump_scaled.py`): the catalog entry compiles to the `bump_scaled` cause, which the simulator finds and validates; its 36 Latin-hypercube draws cover the size ranges and reach the area with the dome's edge, fixed by the seed; each repetition spawns the catalog dome scaled to the drawn size, on the floor; and run as production runs a cause (a separate process answering through a pipe), every repetition returns the size and centre it drew. | pybullet |
| `test_inner_simulator_waits_for_batch.py` | (~1 s) The inner simulator's own `check_for_problems` on simulated DSR graphs: while the semantic agent's `unexplained` intention node exists and no batch is out, the closed recording waits (up to `Simulation.BatchWaitSeconds`, 120 s) instead of running the static causes; a batch in time is simulated alone, no batch gives the static causes once the wait is over, no intention node gives them at once, and a later batch is still simulated. | PySide6, pydsr, Ice (skipped without them) |
